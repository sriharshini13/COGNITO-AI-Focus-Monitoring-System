What COGNITO Does
Here's everything COGNITO monitors in real-time using just your webcam.
👁️

Eye Activity Monitoring

Tracks blink rate and eye openness (EAR) to detect drowsiness and fatigue in real-time.

😮

Yawn Detection

Detects yawning using mouth aspect ratio (MAR) and triggers fatigue alerts immediately.

👀

Gaze & Head Tracking

Monitors iris position and head orientation to measure screen attention and focus direction.

🧠

AI Focus Classification

Random Forest model trained on 9,000+ clips classifies your focus as High, Medium, or Low every frame.

📊

Session Analytics

Every session is saved. View trends, recommendations, and progress across days, weeks and months.
